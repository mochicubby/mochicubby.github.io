Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mochi Cubby ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with the Picki Land mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Content you create in the App, including pools, options, categories, weights, draw results and history, and nicknames, is stored locally on your device, together with your App settings. We do not operate user accounts or servers for this content, and we do not receive, access or store it.

2. Information We Collect

The App uses third-party services that may collect the following information automatically:

  • Identifiers: the device advertising identifier (IDFA), only where you have granted tracking permission and only for advertising; and app-instance and installation identifiers used by Firebase.
  • Usage data: which App features are used, such as opening a screen, starting a draw or changing a setting; interactions with advertisements, including whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end; and steps in the purchase flow, such as opening the PRO page, the plan selected, whether a free trial applies and the result of a purchase or restore.
  • Purchase status: whether PRO is active on your device.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs, non-fatal error reports, performance data, device model and operating system version.
  • Location: approximate location derived from your IP address. The App does not request or collect precise location.

These services do not receive the names of your pools, options or categories, your nicknames, your draw content or results, or any other text you enter in the App. Usage data is limited to fixed values, counts and ranges, such as a range of how many options a pool has.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to display advertising, including personalized advertising where permitted; to deliver the rewards of rewarded ads; to measure advertising performance; to understand how the App and its PRO features are used and improve them; and to diagnose and resolve technical issues.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following services provided by Google LLC:

  • Google AdMob, for banner, native and rewarded advertising.
  • Firebase Analytics, for usage analytics. Analytics does not use the IDFA, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and its ad personalization signals are turned off.
  • Firebase Crashlytics, for crash and error reporting. Error reports include only the type of operation and error involved, not your content.

Information collected by these services is processed and retained by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. Further information is available at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. In-App Purchases

Picki Land PRO is offered as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases, subscription renewals, cancellations and refunds are processed by Apple through the App Store, in accordance with the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your payment information, such as card details. The App checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock PRO features; we do not operate a server for purchases. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.

6. Advertising and Tracking

If you use the free version of the App, it displays banner and native ads. You may choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock weight adjustment for a pool for a limited time or to add a pool slot; watching is always optional. If PRO is active, the App does not request or display any ads.

Before the App first requests ads, it asks once for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework to access the IDFA. If you decline, the App still displays ads without the IDFA, which may be less personalized, and all features remain available. PRO users are not asked. You may change this choice at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.

You may also manage advertising preferences through your device settings and your Google ad settings. You may stop further collection by uninstalling the App, which also deletes the content stored on your device.

7. Availability

The App is not offered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland.

8. Data Security and International Transfers

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Information handled by third-party service providers may be processed in countries other than your own.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Policy, please contact us at mochicubby.support@gmail.com.
