Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mochi Cubby ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with the Picki Land mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Content you create in the App, including pools, options, categories, draw history and nicknames, is stored locally on your device. We do not operate user accounts or servers for this content, and we do not receive, access or store it.

2. Information We Collect

The App uses third-party services that may collect the following information automatically:

  • Identifiers: the device advertising identifier (IDFA), where you have granted tracking permission, and other device or app-instance identifiers.
  • Usage data: interactions with App features and with advertisements.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs, performance data, device model and operating system version.
  • Location: approximate location derived from your IP address. The App does not request or collect precise location.

We do not collect the names, options or other text you enter in the App through these services.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to display advertising, including personalized advertising where permitted; to measure advertising performance; to understand how the App is used and improve it; and to diagnose and resolve technical issues.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following services provided by Google LLC:

  • Google AdMob, for advertising.
  • Firebase Analytics, for usage analytics. Analytics does not use the IDFA.
  • Firebase Crashlytics, for crash reporting.

Information collected by these services is processed and retained by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. Further information is available at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

5. Tracking and Your Choices

The App requests your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing the IDFA. If you decline, advertisements may still be displayed but will be less personalized. You may change this choice at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.

You may also manage advertising preferences through your device settings and your Google ad settings. You may stop further collection by uninstalling the App, which also deletes the content stored on your device.

6. Data Security and International Transfers

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Information handled by third-party service providers may be processed in countries other than your own.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Policy, please contact us at mochicubby.support@gmail.com.
