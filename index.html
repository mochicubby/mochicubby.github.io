About Mochi Cubby

Mochi Cubby is a cozy little corner built by soft, squishy creatures. We make small, handy apps for everyday life, each with an interface that feels as comfortable as sinking into a warm cushion.

We hope every tap feels gentle, every screen feels calm, and every app makes your day a little easier.

  • ☁️Soft and friendlyRounded, gentle designs with little friends to keep you company.
  • 👆Simple to useGet it done in a few taps, with no extra steps.
  • 🔒Your data stays yoursWhat you create stays on your device or in your own iCloud.

Decision helper
